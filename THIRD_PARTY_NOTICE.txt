ms-swift
--------

GARR includes modified, installable ms-swift source under `src/ms-swift/`.
It is derived from upstream commit
`6d0bcfba8ce7a1dedfd20e1ac8fc887bb16619a0`.

The GARR modifications add HDF5-backed image loading, the Generative Alignment
objective, hidden-state forwarding required by that objective, and batched
generated-score and representation export. Modified files carry a file-level
notice. The complete change inventory is recorded in
`src/ms-swift/GARR_MODIFICATIONS.md`.
